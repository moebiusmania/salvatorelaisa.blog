The order of the evening

Six things will happen

Everything runs to the hour, except the séance, which runs to whatever answers.

🍸 20:30 — The cellar

The cauldron bar

Smoking negronis, a blood-orange punch, and one drink nobody will admit to mixing. Soft options on the left of the table.

🎃 21:00 — Courtyard

Carve-off

Pumpkins and knives provided. Judged on fear, not craft. Bring your own design if you're serious about winning.

🔮 22:15 — Library

The séance

Madame Corvi reads the cards by candlelight. Twelve chairs, no photographs, and nobody leaves the circle early.

💀 23:00 — Ballroom

Costume judging

One rule: nothing bought whole from a shop. The winner takes the urn on the mantelpiece, contents included.

🕯️ 00:00 — Everywhere

Lights out

Every bulb in the house goes off for ten minutes. Candles are handed out at the door. Keep hold of yours.

🦇 00:10 till 4:00

Dancing

DJ Nocturne plays until the neighbours file the complaint. Last tram is at 02:00 — after that, walk in pairs.
